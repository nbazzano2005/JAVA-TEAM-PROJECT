# Story guide — Presentation Edition (spoilers)

Eight decisions cover asking a question, choosing a study method, explaining an idea, supporting a teammate, presenting a project, choosing rest, preparing outside the exam room, and handling a changed exam assumption.

Three chapter introductions + eight choice scenes + 24 choice-response scenes + one results scene + five endings = **41 scenes total**. A single run visits **21 scenes** and makes eight story decisions plus the final results action.

## Ending rules, evaluated in order

| Priority | Condition | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Knowledge below 8 OR Confidence below 4 | Begin Again / Fail |
| 2 | Energy below 1 | Burnout / Fail |
| 3 | Support at least 5 | Together / Pass |
| 4 | Knowledge at least 14 AND Confidence at least 8 AND Energy at least 3 | Distinction / Pass with distinction |
| 5 | All remaining cases meeting the pass requirements | Forward / Pass |

Start with Energy 4 and all other stats zero. Support takes priority over Distinction so the endings reflect different kinds of success. The whiteboard extension requires Support 3. The strongest exam answer requires Knowledge 8.
